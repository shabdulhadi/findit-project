How it works

01

File a report

Lost something or found something? Add a few details and a photo if you have one — it only takes a couple minutes.

02

We look for a match

FindIt compares descriptions, locations, and photos across every report on campus to spot likely matches.

03

Get notified

When there's a good match, both people get an email so they can arrange to reconnect and recover the item.
