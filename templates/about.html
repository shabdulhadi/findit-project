About FindIt

Reuniting people with what they've lost

FindIt is a student-built lost & found network connecting every campus in the University of the Punjab system.

What is FindIt?

FindIt is a campus lost & found recovery platform built by students, for students. Instead of relying on flyers, WhatsApp groups, or word of mouth, FindIt gives every campus a single place to report a lost item or a found item — and automatically matches the two based on category, location, description, and photo.

Which campuses does it cover?

FindIt currently serves the following campuses across the University of the Punjab network:

  • PUCIT Old Campus
  • PUCIT New Campus
  • University of the Punjab
  • Johar Town Campus
  • PUCAD
  • Hailey College
  • Oriental College
  • Pharmacy Department

How it works

Report

Report a lost or found item, on your campus.

Match

We help match items with their owners automatically.

Reunite

Items get returned. Smiles are shared.

Stay safe

Always meet in visible, public campus areas when returning or collecting an item.
